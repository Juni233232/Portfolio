    Unit 1 · Fitts's law

    Touch target sizer

    Bigger, nearer targets are easier to hit. Apple suggests about 44 pt, Material Design 48 dp.

    Register

    Verdict

    One CSS pixel is close to one point on a phone. Treat the guideline as a minimum, and add space between targets.

    Unit 1 · Hick's law

    More choices, more thinking

    Decision time grows with the number of options. Drag to add menu items to Gather's navigation.

    Relative effort, log₂(n + 1)

    A simple model, not a stopwatch. Real timing depends on familiarity, grouping and layout.

    How it is designed

    Design you can check.

    The site borrows the deck's monochrome system and adds motion. Both follow the same accessibility rules, and the numbers below are computed live.

    The deck

    Monochrome and condensed

    • Anton headlines in two tones: ink first, then grey.
    • Grey rounded tiles with white pill labels.
    • A three-dot marker and a pill tag on every slide.
    • Greyscale throughout, so the Gather mockups carry the only colour.

    The site

    Editorial motion

    • A loading sequence, smooth scrolling and a pinned sideways journey.
    • Word-by-word reveals and a hover preview on every topic.
    • Press feedback starts on touch, before release.
    • Reduced motion keeps every moment as a gentle fade.

    Both

    Accessible by default

    • Contrast of 4.5:1 for small text, 3:1 for large text.
    • The deck's light grey (#CDCDCD) is 1.6:1 on white, so the site uses #6E6E6E for its second tone.
    • Touch targets of at least 44 px, and meaning never carried by colour alone.
    • Visible labels, keyboard support and clear focus rings.
